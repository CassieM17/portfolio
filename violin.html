Violin

Cassandra ("Cassie") May holds a dual degree in Systems Design and Violin Performance from Carnegie Mellon University, where she studied under William van der Sloot. Born and raised in the Bay Area, she began her violin studies with Patricia Burnham and David Chernyavsky.

Cassie has earned top prizes in local and international competitions and was recently awarded the Harry G. Archer Award at Carnegie Mellon. She has attended the Boston University Tanglewood Institute, Bowdoin International Music Festival, and Mozarteum University in Salzburg. Her string quartet, Mezza, recently won the Pittsburgh Concert Society Major Competition.

Cassie also teaches violin to students ages 8 to 20, helping them with solo and orchestral repertoire as well as orchestra auditions.

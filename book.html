Your private library

Timeframe2026
Projectbooki
RoleDesigner, Front-End Developer

Summary

Booki is a quiet, private bookshelf to log what you're reading, with no feeds and no followers.

What I Did

I designed and built the front end end-to-end: a searchable shelf sortable by author, genre, title, date added, or TBR; and an add-a-book form with optional genre tags and a backdatable "date added" field, so a shelf can reflect books read long before you started using the app.
